# PostingGTA Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Game footage is the loudest raw material on the internet, and most of it never leaves a hard drive. PostingGTA exists to close that gap: find the best beat in a long session, shape it into a short vertical post, and put it on every feed with as little friction as possible.

Mission: make posting game moments faster than playing them, and keep the community's scoreboard public.

## Roadmap

1. **Open the board.** Public site, live Solana market board, pinned $POSTING slot, social presence.
2. **Ship the studio.** Moment scoring, word-timed captions and vertical export in early access.
3. **Turn on rewards.** Publish pool rules, open the creator lane, add one-click cross-posting.
4. **Widen the loop.** Drop-folder import for recordings, more caption looks, community-proposed features.

Phases describe intent and order, not dates or guarantees.
